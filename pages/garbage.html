Our Story

Building Reliable Supply Chains Across Global Markets.

REISAGRI was established with a clear objective—to simplify international agricultural trade through dependable sourcing, transparent business practices and efficient supply chain management. Based in New Delhi, India, we work closely with trusted producers and processing partners to supply premium agricultural commodities to buyers around the world.

We understand that successful commodity trading depends on consistency, quality and timely execution. Every shipment is managed with careful attention to sourcing, quality assurance, documentation and logistics, ensuring our customers receive products that meet international expectations.

At REISAGRI, we believe long-term partnerships are built through trust rather than transactions. Our commitment to integrity, operational excellence and customer-focused service enables us to create lasting value for importers, distributors and businesses across global markets.

Leadership

Driven by Experience. Guided by Integrity.

REISAGRI is led by Izhar Alam, Chief Executive Officer, whose experience in agricultural commodity trading and international supply chain management has helped shape the company's commitment to reliability, transparency and operational excellence.

With more than a decade of industry experience, he has worked across sourcing, procurement, risk management and international trade, developing strong relationships with producers, logistics partners and buyers across multiple markets.

His leadership philosophy is simple: build long-term partnerships through honesty, consistent quality and dependable execution. Under his guidance, REISAGRI continues to strengthen its position as a trusted sourcing partner for agricultural commodities worldwide.
