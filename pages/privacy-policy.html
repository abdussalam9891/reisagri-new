Privacy Policy

At REISAGRI, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and safeguard personal and business information when you use our website or communicate with our team.

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you contact REISAGRI, request a quotation, submit an enquiry or conduct business with us, we may collect information necessary to provide our services and manage our business relationship.

  • Full Name
  • Company Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Business Address
  • Country of Operation
  • Product Enquiries & Purchase Requirements
  • Import & Export Documentation (when required)

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect enables us to deliver reliable sourcing, export and customer support services while maintaining efficient communication with our business partners.

  • Respond to enquiries and quotation requests.
  • Process export orders and business transactions.
  • Prepare invoices and shipping documentation.
  • Coordinate logistics and international deliveries.
  • Improve our products, services and website.
  • Comply with legal, regulatory and trade obligations.

3. Business Communications

We may communicate with you by email, telephone, WhatsApp or other appropriate business channels regarding enquiries, quotations, order status, shipping updates, documentation, invoices and other information directly related to our services.

Where permitted by law, we may occasionally send information about new products, sourcing opportunities or company updates. You may opt out of these communications at any time.

4. Cookies & Analytics

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to improve website performance, understand visitor behaviour and provide a better browsing experience. Cookies may also help us remember your preferences for future visits.

You may choose to disable cookies through your browser settings. However, certain features of our website may not function as intended.

5. Sharing Your Information

REISAGRI does not sell or rent your personal or business information to third parties. Information is shared only when necessary to provide our services or fulfil legal obligations.

  • Shipping and logistics partners.
  • Customs and regulatory authorities.
  • Banks and payment service providers.
  • Technology and website service providers.
  • Professional advisers where legally required.

6. International Data Transfers

As an international agricultural trading company, REISAGRI may exchange business information with customers, suppliers, logistics providers and service partners located in different countries.

Whenever information is transferred across borders, we take reasonable measures to ensure it is handled securely and in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

7. Data Security

REISAGRI implements appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal and business information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. Access to sensitive information is limited to authorized personnel who require it to perform their responsibilities.

While we continually work to maintain secure systems and business processes, no method of electronic transmission or online storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your Rights

Subject to applicable laws, you may request access to, correction of or deletion of the personal information we hold about you. You may also request that we update inaccurate information or withdraw consent for non-essential business communications where applicable.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

REISAGRI may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our business practices, legal obligations or website functionality. The latest version will always be published on this page together with the effective date.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or how REISAGRI handles your information, please contact us.
